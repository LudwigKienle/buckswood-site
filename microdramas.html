The case

A two-minute series a day, made with AI.

Buckswood Studios writes, casts, shoots and cuts every episode in its own AI pipeline. No sets, no crew, and a new episode every day.

Story

A familiar trope, a hook in the first two seconds and a cliffhanger at the end.

Cast

Invented characters with fixed faces and wardrobe sheets, so every episode matches the last.

Location

One reference image per set keeps the room, light and layout the same from shot to shot.

Shoot

Video generation with Seedance 2.5: drafts at low resolution, then the chosen take finished at 1080p.

Cut & publish

Edited, mixed and released in 9:16 and 16:9 on YouTube, Facebook and Instagram.

Who watches

Mostly viewers over 55 in the US, the UK, Canada and Australia. About nine in ten views come straight from the Shorts feed, not from subscribers.

What holds them

Respect, karma and a reveal. The pilots run two minutes and are still watched to the end, because each scene raises the stakes.

What brings them back

A simple vote at the end: "Fire him or give him a second chance?" The audience picks the next episode, and the comments fill up.
